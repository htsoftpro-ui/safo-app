# About Figures
These are figures used for the paper "Clustering analysis of metered Arabic poetry compositions". There are 6 files. One for data exploration and five for poems assignment to clusters.

boxplot_data: boxplots of clustering scaled data

opt_1_pot_rep: figure for clustering option by the two metrics POT and REP

opt_2_rep_div: figure for clustering option by the two metrics REP and DIV

opt_3_6: figure for clustering option by the two metrics REP and EVE and by USG and EVE

opt_4_5_rep_var: figure for clustering options by the two metrics REP and VAR

opt_7_8_usg_rep: figure for clustering options by the two metrics USG and REP


# About collection methodology
These figures are generated by R plots based on clustering options.

# Files formats
These results are provided in Encapsulated PostScript format (EPS) and Portable Network Graphics (PNG) format.
